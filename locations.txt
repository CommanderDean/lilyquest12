Locations & Audio Scripts for Lily's Quest (12th Birthday, 2026)
==================================================================

BALLOON METHOD: codes are hidden inside/on balloons this year — some fully
inflated, some barely blown up, some not inflated at all. How each one is
revealed (pop it, it's just deflated, or the code's written on the outside)
can vary spot by spot — no fixed rule, decide on the day.

Door 2 (the pool) is sorted: balloon tied to a rock via string, dropped in
the middle. No more waterproofing worries there.

==================================================
Your 10 Hiding Spots
==================================================

1. Behind the standing mirror in the upstairs hallway
   Code: 2468

   Clue 1 (Clue1.mp3):
   "Upstairs stands a silver guardian who shows you your own face each day,
    yet she has never once spoken her own name. Step behind her, young
    seeker, and see what she has been hiding from you."


2. At the bottom of the pool in the backyard
   Code: 7351

   Clue 2 (Clue2.mp3):
   "In the backyard lies a realm of blue, still and deep, where you cannot
    breathe but the fish would feel at home. Take a breath, brave seeker,
    and dive down to where the sunlight fades — your next secret rests
    upon the floor of the deep."


3. In the hanging basket on the front porch (empty, hangs from the ceiling)
   Code: 4812

   Clue 3 (Clue3.mp3):
   "At the front of the house hangs a vessel meant for flowers and leaves,
    yet no green thing grows within it now. Reach up into its emptiness,
    seeker, and you shall find what waits."


4. In the pocket of her pink puffer jacket, in her room
   Code: 6237

   Clue 4 (Clue4.mp3):
   "Somewhere in your own chamber rests a coat as pink as dawn, puffed up
    like a cloud caught mid-fall. Search its pocket, young seeker, and
    your next secret shall be found."


5. In the esky with the Australian flag design, downstairs outside
   Code: 1584

   Clue 5 (Clue5.mp3):
   "Outside, below, a box stands guard — one that keeps cold things cold,
    marked with the stars of our southern sky. Open its lid, seeker, and
    claim what lies within."


6. In the spider skeleton's rib cage — Halloween toy, with Lincoln's toys
   Code: 5813

   Clue 6 (Clue6.mp3):
   "Among your brother's treasures crawls a creature of eight legs and
    bare bones, who wakes each year when pumpkins glow. Look within its
    bony chest, seeker, for that is where your secret hides."


7. In the big drum (djembe) with the musical instruments
   Code: 3175

   Clue 7 (Clue7.mp3):
   "Among the instruments that sing throughout your home stands one that
    speaks not with strings or reeds, but with the beat of open hands upon
    its skin. Look deep inside its round wooden belly for what you seek."


8. Behind the Lord of the Rings books, upstairs bookcase
   Code: 6284

   Clue 8 (Clue8.mp3):
   "On the upstairs shelf lives a tale of a small hero who carried a great
    and terrible ring on a long and dangerous road. Move the story of that
    journey aside, seeker, and find what has been hidden behind it."


9. Up high in the towel cupboard (look straight up when inside)
   Code: 7182

   Clue 9 (Clue9.mp3):
   "Return once more to the cupboard of soft folded things, where warmth
    is kept for after the bath. This time, do not look ahead — lift your
    eyes to the heavens above, seeker, and there your secret waits."


10. In Dad's shoe
    Code: 4861

    Clue 10 (Clue10.mp3):
    "Even a crystal ball must borrow help from mortal things. Somewhere
     nearby rests a single shoe belonging to one who walks always by your
     side. Search inside it, seeker, and your very last secret shall be
     revealed."

Reaction Audio — REUSE FROM LINCOLN'S VERSION, NO RE-RECORDING NEEDED
==================================================
These never mention a name, so you can copy the .mp3 files straight across from
Lincoln's audio folder into this project's audio folder and be done with them.

Coughs (cough1.mp3 – cough4.mp3)
    [Plays when Lily taps the orb while audio is already playing — interrupts the clue]
    cough1: one short sharp cough
    cough2: two coughs in a row
    cough3: a long drawn-out "ahem"
    cough4: a cough followed by a grumble

Nopes (nope1.mp3 – nope7.mp3)
    [Plays when a wrong code is entered — keep each one short]
    nope1: "Hmm... that is not correct."
    nope2: "No, no, no. Try again."
    nope3: "Not quite, young seeker. Look more carefully."
    nope4: "That is not right. Think again."
    nope5: "The door remains locked. Try once more."
    nope6: "Incorrect! You can do it — keep trying."
    nope7: "Not yet... I believe in you. Try again."

Also reusable as pure sound effects (no voice): click.mp3, unlock.mp3
