Locations & Audio Scripts for Lily's Quest (12th Birthday, 2026)
==================================================================

BALLOON METHOD: codes are hidden inside/on balloons this year — some fully
inflated, some barely blown up, some not inflated at all. How each one is
revealed (pop it, it's just deflated, or the code's written on the outside)
can vary spot by spot — no fixed rule, decide on the day.

Door 2 (the pool) is sorted: balloon tied to a rock via string, dropped in
the middle. No more waterproofing worries there.

==================================================

   Code: 2468

   Clue 1 (Clue1.mp3):
   "The first clue! There's a girl who stands silent in the hallway. Whenever you look at her, she returns your gaze. What is she hiding behind her back. Tucked away. She will not say."

2. Up high in the towel cupboard (look straight up when inside)
   Code: 7182

   Clue 2 (Clue2.mp3):

   Code: 4812

   Clue 3 (Clue3.mp3):

   Code: 1584

8. Behind the Lord of the Rings books, upstairs bookcase

   Code: 7351

   Clue 9 (Clue9.mp3):

Reaction Audio — REUSE FROM LINCOLN'S VERSION, NO RE-RECORDING NEEDED
==================================================
These never mention a name, so you can copy the .mp3 files straight across from
Lincoln's audio folder into this project's audio folder and be done with them.

Coughs (cough1.mp3 – cough4.mp3)
    [Plays when Lily taps the orb while audio is already playing — interrupts the clue]
    cough1: one short sharp cough
    cough2: two coughs in a row
    cough3: a long drawn-out "ahem"
    cough4: a cough followed by a grumble

Nopes (nope1.mp3 – nope7.mp3)
    [Plays when a wrong code is entered — keep each one short]
    nope1: "Hmm... that is not correct."
    nope2: "No, no, no. Try again."
    nope3: "Not quite, young seeker. Look more carefully."
    nope4: "That is not right. Think again."
    nope5: "The door remains locked. Try once more."
    nope6: "Incorrect! You can do it — keep trying."
    nope7: "Not yet... I believe in you. Try again."

Also reusable as pure sound effects (no voice): click.mp3, unlock.mp3
